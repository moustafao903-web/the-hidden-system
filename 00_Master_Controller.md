- Output clarity

- Promise alignment  
- Series identity fit  
- Transfer readiness  
- Amazon optimization potential

## Failure Modes
- Weak handoff  
- Overlap of responsibilities  
- Generic outputs  
- Misalignment between title/outline/cover  
- Poor commercial fit  
- Inconsistent tone  
- Excessive complexity  
- Outputs unusable downstream

- Revision must happen at origin, not at end.  
- Controller enforces recursive refinement until stability.
